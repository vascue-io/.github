Vascue is a Hong Kong health tech company building an AI front desk for clinics and hospitals. It answers patient enquiries, books appointments, and prepares insurance claims — 24/7, in 15+ languages — so care teams can focus on patients, not process.

## What Vascue does

- **24/7 multilingual patient messaging** — answers enquiries over WhatsApp in 15+ languages, with no wait times
- **Seamless scheduling** — bookings land directly in Cliniko, Nookal, Jane, or Vascue's built-in calendar; no double entry, no overbooking
- **Quotation & claims automation** — instant treatment quotes and insurance claims prepared automatically
- **PDF & image analysis** — reads referral letters, claim forms, and documents patients send in chat
- **Staff dashboard oversight** — humans stay in control, with insight into what slows the clinic down

## Who it's for

- [Physiotherapy clinics](https://www.vascue.io/solutions/physiotherapy) — stay in the treatment room while Vascue handles enquiries, bookings, and claims
- [Hospitals & departments](https://www.vascue.io/solutions/hospitals) — department-scale patient communication on privacy-first architecture
- [Allied health](https://www.vascue.io/solutions/allied-health) — chiropractic, podiatry, TCM, and other practices

Vascue is a health tech company building the operational spine for clinics and hospitals. From the first message to the final claim, Vascue runs the front desk and the operations behind it — so care teams can focus on patients, not process.

## How it works — the Vascue loop

1. **Triage** *(live)* — a WhatsApp front desk that answers, triages, books, and quotes, 24/7 and multilingual. It reads referral letters, clinical notes, and lab results, so every inquiry reaches the right team.
2. **Vision** *(in development)* — every visit becomes a stream of events: arrival, in-room, completion, report status. Vascue turns those events into action, surfacing the bottlenecks you couldn't see.
3. **Resolve** *(live)* — from referral photo to finished claim in about a minute: pricing, fee-code mapping, and claim forms generate automatically, with every conversation and visit feeding the next decision.

## What's in the system

- **Front desk** — 24/7 multilingual support, smart scheduling into Cliniko or Nookal, instant quotes from your fee schedule, referral & image reading, batch processing, staff override & control
- **Claims** — quotation & claim forms, fee-code mapping, claim submission & tracking, pre-auth automation, shortfall flagging, payment reconciliation
- **Oversight** — dashboard oversight, patient journey monitoring, see-through analytics

## Results — live production numbers

- **110,000+** messages handled
- **99.2%** replies rated correct by staff
- **~60s** from referral photo to quote
